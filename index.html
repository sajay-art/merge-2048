Game Rules & Manual

  • Select Tile: Tap any tile to select it (a white border appears).
  • Nearby Moves Only: Move or merge into adjacent spaces (Up, Down, Left, Right).
  • No Diagonal Jumps: You cannot jump across tiles or move diagonally.
  • Merge Rules: Tap matching nearby numbers (2+2, 4+4) to merge.

How to Play

  • Tap a tile to highlight it with a white border.
  • Tap an empty adjacent slot to move there.
  • Tap an adjacent matching tile to merge them.
  • Diagonal moves are not allowed.
